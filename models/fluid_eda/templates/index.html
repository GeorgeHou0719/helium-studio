Solver settings
? Model assumptions

Steady helium network with independently solved pipe segments. Conduction between components is excluded. Gas carries energy through the circuit. Pot cooling is calculated from incoming feed enthalpy and outgoing saturated vapor; node temperatures remain prescribed trial boundaries. Pipe walls may be adiabatic or have a prescribed temperature.

COMSOL solves an axisymmetric model for each pipe. Quick preview uses a 1D model. Two-phase continuous pot solves the five-component example loop using public helium properties, finite heat exchange and a homogeneous equilibrium capillary. It is an engineering model; slip, film flow and superfluid channel resistance are not resolved. Check result warnings and model help.
